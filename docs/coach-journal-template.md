## Rolling signals (fill as they appear)

- **Trust:** which AI suggestions they accept without edit vs override, and why.
- **Time:** any concrete "that saved me N minutes" moments.
- **Stickiness:** what they'd miss most if it disappeared tomorrow.
- **Churn risk:** any "I switched back to ___" moments.

## How this feeds the roadmap

- ⚠️ friction/quotes → a row on the Beta Feedback Board (severity + area).
- Repeated asks across ≥ multiple coaches → a candidate through the three-question
  gate. One coach's wish is a note; three coaches' shared friction is a sprint.
